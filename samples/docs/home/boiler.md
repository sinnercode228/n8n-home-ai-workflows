# Boiler (fictional demo document)

The boiler is a Veritherm C30 combi in the utility room. It heats the radiators and the hot water.

## Annual service

The boiler needs an annual service every September or October. Last service: 2025-10-02 by Brightwell Heating. Warranty requires a service at least every 13 months, so do not skip a year.

## Pressure

Normal pressure is 1.0-1.5 bar when cold. If it drops below 0.8 bar, top up using the filling loop under the boiler. If it keeps dropping, call Brightwell Heating instead of topping up again.

## Error codes

F1 means low pressure (see above). F7 means the flame did not light: check the gas is on, then press reset once. Anything else: call the engineer.
